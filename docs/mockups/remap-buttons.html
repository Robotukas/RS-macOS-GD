Remap: any button can send any pad button

Version 2, after your two comments: every button on the controller can be remapped, not only the extra ones, and a mapped button is only what it is mapped to: whatever it did by itself, such as changing the volume, is switched off.

1 · Main screen changed

Clicking any control opens a small panel with two separate things: what the button sends to games, and which physical button it is. Learning starts only from "Re-learn…". A control that sends something other than its default shows it on the label.

2 · A mapped button does only what it is mapped to

Some buttons already do something by themselves. When you remap one, GDHub switches that off. A button you leave alone keeps behaving as it does today.

ButtonWhat it does by itselfWhen you remap it
G2, G3, G4Change the volume (down, mute, up). Being confirmed by the recording.That key's volume function is off. The other two keep it unless you remap them too.
G1, G5Nothing known.Sends what you chose.
Start (centre)GDHub sends it as the Xbox Guide button today. Holding it also switches the controller off; that is inside the controller and stays.Sends what you chose instead of Guide. Guide stays available as a target for any button.
Paddles (4)The controller itself turns them into D-pad right, down, up, left.GDHub hides that D-pad press while the paddle is held and sends your choice instead.
SAX left, rightThe controller itself turns them into A and B.GDHub hides that A or B press while the SAX button is held and sends your choice instead.
ProfileSwitches between the controller's own stored profiles. To be checked: this happens inside the controller.Sends what you chose. If the profile switch cannot be stopped from the Mac, the panel says so.
A, B, X, Y, LB, RB, View, Menu, L3, R3, D-padSend themselves.Send what you chose instead. Swapping two buttons is two remaps: A sends B, B sends A.

Re-learn any controlNo change to what it sendsOnly "Physical button" changes
  • Saved with the mapping and applied live, the same way a learn edit is applied today. "Default" on one control and "Reset all remaps" undo it.
  • The same on both paths. The engine and the driver run the same translator, so a remap behaves identically whether the app or, later, the driver drives the pad.
  • Sticks and trigger travel stay as they are in this feature. LT and RT can be chosen as a target ("full press") and can be swapped with each other, keeping their travel.
  • Limit on the driver path: a pad button can be fed by two physical buttons at most, for example "Y or G1". With three, it still works, but GDHub drives the pad from the app and says so:
    Input path: App (mapping needs GDHM v2: three buttons send Y)

5 · Build order I recommend

StepCoversWhy in this order
1 firstAll ordinary buttons, G1–G5, Start and Profile, with the volume switched off for a mapped G2–G4Uses only what the translator can already do, plus the volume switch-off.
2 right afterPaddles and SAXHiding the controller's own D-pad or A/B press needs one recording first, to see whether the paddle and its built-in press arrive at the same instant. It also needs a driver update.

6 · Decisions for you

A. What should a click on a control do? Recommended: open the small panel (as drawn). Learning starts only from "Re-learn…".
B. Build in the two steps above? Recommended: yes. Everything is remappable at the end; step 1 gives you G1 → Y and the rest of the front buttons first.
C. Should the target light up too? Recommended: yes, dimmer, so you can see on screen that G1 is producing Y.
