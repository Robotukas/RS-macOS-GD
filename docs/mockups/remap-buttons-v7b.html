The limits. Most of what you accepted on 10 November (Q3) is gone: a button pressed at the same moment as the key now passes at once, LT and RT are never touched, and pressing Profile during a hold is no longer a limit by itself. What is left:

  • The key's own button. While you hold a remapped key, pressing that same button yourself shows only when you let go of the key. If you let go of that button first, it is lost. A button you were already holding stays on until you let go of the key.
  • New: both paddles of a pair, remapped, held together (both outer paddles, or both inner paddles). The controller itself then sends no left or right (or no up or down). The pad keeps that direction as it was just before you pressed the pair, until you let go of one of them. The other direction works. (Q10)
  • New: after a Profile press changed the button the controller adds for a key. GDHub notices at your next press of that key and hides the new button too. In four cases it cannot notice, and the new button can show next to your pick until you let go of the key: you press two remapped keys in the very same instant; you were already holding the new button or the key's old button; another button or direction changes in the very same instant; GDHub restarts its reading while you hold the key. (Q11)
  • Rare: a direction opposite to the paddle's, pressed in the very same instant as the paddle (or already held when GDHub starts reading, reconnects or wakes while the paddle is down), shows only when you let go of the paddle.

Other new Developer-mode wording

  • A hiding check can say one of three things instead of a pass:
    • cannot judge: the controller's current profile sends A for this key (A is an example). It appears when the button the controller adds for the key is the same as your pick, a button held in the background, or the button you press during the hold.
    • cannot judge: the controller's button for this key was not seen at its press (it sent none or several). It appears when the press could not be read.
    • not exercised (the controller sent no D-pad right during the hold). It appears when the key's own button never reached the receiver during the hold, for example because the controller cancelled it against a held opposite direction.
  • A "one button per press" step for an untouched key where the controller sent no button: cannot judge, not a fail. Its tooltip: the controller sent no button for this key.
  • Something held in the background that misbehaves: A dropped while View was being pressed (fell 2 times before the last View release) · A came up after View · A went down 2 times (needs 1).
  • G2, G3, G4 keep their wording: needs G2 left at its built-in function: Reset it in Mapping.
  • Choosing Driver on build 24 for a mapping it cannot take names keys in words: Driver pass cannot be confirmed: Paddle outer right sends R3: this driver version cannot hide the controller's own press. Reset that remap first.
